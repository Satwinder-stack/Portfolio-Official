Smart Parking System

May-Jun 2025

  • HTML5
  • CSS3
  • JavaScript
  • MySQL
  • PHP

This is a fully functional web-based system where users can avail & reserve spots in a parking area at specific time they want freely. It updates real-time and allows customer to know specific spots in advance. It also allows the customer to reserve a whole spot for up to a whole day. There are also rules in-between availing or reserving a spot whether it would be reserving a spot 10 minutes after a customer just used it - which is not allowed. Or simply if a customer doesn't remove their car from that parking spot after their time is up, the physical staff will be handling the matter. Future implementations will still be added to satisfy each and every consumer needs.

Car Rental System

Mar 2026

  • HTML5
  • CSS3
  • TypeScript
  • Angular
  • NoSQL
  • MongoDB

This is a fully developed car rental system designed to streamline the entire booking and management process, featuring real-time vehicle availability tracking that allows users to view accurate and up-to-date information when selecting from a diverse fleet of vehicles; it also incorporates a 24-hour maintenance buffer logic that automatically reserves downtime between rentals for inspection and servicing, ensuring vehicle readiness and reliability, while a built-in simulated digital wallet enables secure and seamless transactions, all supported by a custom administrative approval workflow that gives administrators full control over reviewing, validating, and managing booking requests to maintain a structured and efficient rental operation.
