My Story

A few chapters on how I got here, what I work with, and where I'm heading.

How I Started

I started as a student with barely any knowledge in my freshman year due to not being on a specialized strand focusing on programming. I started off as clueless like everyone else in programming, especially once it reaches multiple pages. There was even a time that I refused to acknowledge using multiple files due to its complexity. However, I persevered to study everything and participated in coding competitions. Here I am today, being able to handle complex projects.

Tech Skills

I use HTML and CSS to build dynamic, user-friendly, and responsive interfaces, and JavaScript to bring them to life with interactive features. I'm well-versed in Python and Java for data science and logic-heavy programs. I also work with Node.js, React, Angular, Vue.js, Figma, and WordPress. On the infrastructure side, I handle hardware diagnostics and repair, Windows and Linux administration, network troubleshooting, endpoint lifecycle management, and deployment scripting. Lastly, I have solid skills in cybersecurity, SEO, and handling data securely.

My Goals

Expand my skills by experiencing how programming is used in the industry. Furthermore, I want to contribute to the company that has given me the opportunity to work for them by applying what I have been practicing and learning. Lastly, I want to be able to expand my network with other colleagues that I'll be able to work with.

Fun Facts

I enjoy developing systems, not just the frontend, I like developing systems as side projects. Furthermore, I work well in a team and enjoy leading other members, and all throughout the project time I'll try to get to know them. I also work well independently while listening to music to concentrate. Additionally, I can adapt quickly to systems that I'm required to learn. Lastly, I can visualize how data flows through a system before it's built.

Specialization

Currently, I'm specializing in Frontend Development, however, I am able to go full-stack with all my skills. I enjoy creating systems, not just the frontend, it also includes the design, backend, APIs, database, hosting, and server configuration.
